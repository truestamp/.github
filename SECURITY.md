# Security Policy

This policy applies to every repository in the
[Truestamp organization](https://github.com/truestamp) that doesn't have its
own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue, discussion, or pull request for a security report.
Report it privately instead:

1. **GitHub private vulnerability reporting** (preferred), with **Report a
   vulnerability** on the repository's **Security** tab, where the repository
   offers it.
2. **Email** to <security@truestamp.com>, with the name of the affected
   repository or service in the subject line.

Include, where possible:

- The affected repository, version, or URL.
- Steps to reproduce, or a minimal proof of concept.
- The impact you believe a successful exploit would have.

We aim to acknowledge your report within three business days, and to tell you
whether we could reproduce it and what we plan to do. Please give us 90 days
before publishing details, or a shorter window if we agree on one.

Scope, safe harbor, and the rest of the terms are in
[Reporting Security Issues](https://www.truestamp.com/security#reporting) on
truestamp.com. If anything here disagrees with that page, that page applies.
