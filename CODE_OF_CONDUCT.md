# Code of Conduct

<!-- Shared with CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md in truestamp-cli. Keep the two the same, apart from the paragraph saying where it applies and the link to the security policy. -->

Truestamp adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).

It applies to every repository in the [Truestamp organization](https://github.com/truestamp) that doesn't have its own code of conduct, and to the [organization discussions](https://github.com/orgs/truestamp/discussions). By participating, through issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other Truestamp community channel, you agree to abide by its terms.

## Reporting

Report a possible violation to <support@truestamp.com>. Reports are handled confidentially. For security-sensitive issues, follow the [security policy](https://github.com/truestamp/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The Truestamp maintainers act as the Community Moderators the Covenant describes. They investigate every report and decide on any action using the enforcement ladder in its "Addressing and Repairing Harm" section.
